HOW TO PLAY

⚔ CONTROLS

Desktop:

  • Move: WASD or Arrow Keys
  • Light Attack: Left Click or J
  • Heavy Attack: Right Click or K
  • Dodge / Dash: Shift or Space
  • Divine Blade: Key 1
  • Vayu Dash: Key 2
  • Indra Lightning: Key 3
  • Agni Burst: Key 4
  • Gaja Form: Key G
  • Change Camera: Key C
  • Pause: ESC

Mobile: Use the on-screen virtual joystick to move and tap the action buttons on the right to attack, dodge, and cast divine powers.

☸ TEMPLE INTEGRITY

Enemies called Vighnas attack both the Guardian and the sacred temple shrine.

Intercept enemies before they shatter the Sacred Ward! If Temple Integrity reaches 0%, it is Game Over.

🐘 GAJA FORM

Fill the Gaja Meter in battle by landing strikes and defeating foes. Press G to awaken the supernatural elephant-aspect titan with colossal ground slams, shockwaves, and invulnerability!

(Note: Gaja Form is the mortal champion's divine transformation, not Lord Ganesha)

🌊 WAVES & BOSSES

Each of the 5 areas features: Wave 1, Wave 2, Wave 3, Elite Wave, and Boss Encounter. Defeat all 5 bosses to protect the temple!
